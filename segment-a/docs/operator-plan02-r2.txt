A3 DATA PRESERVATION

Runner payloads may contain descriptions of different lengths.

Preserve actual returned content.

Do not require uniform description lengths.

Cleaning may normalize presentation but must not invent facts or erase provenance.

Duplicate handling must retain sufficient identity to trace repeated source records.

The sidecar is the supporting source-evidence corpus.

The envelope is the operational state and navigation artifact.

The envelope references evidence through UIDs and source associations.

A4 STATE MINTING

A4 converts A3's committed decisions into authoritative artifact state.

Only committed PUSH jobs become active downstream selections.

PIN and PENDING remain outside the committed active set.

REJECT records are excluded.

Every active envelope UID must resolve to a corresponding sidecar record.

The envelope must preserve relevant batch and search-scope identity.

The sidecar must preserve evidence required by the existing B1 contract.

Do not assume B1 accepts a particular job cardinality without inspecting its contract.
